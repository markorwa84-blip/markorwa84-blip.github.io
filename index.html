✨ What Does This Tool Do?

Certificates are small digital files that help your computer verify secure connections and software. Windows keeps them in hidden "stores" on your system. This tool simply opens those stores and shows you everything in a clear, friendly way.

  • 🔍 Browse Certificates: See all stored certificates in an organized, searchable list. No confusing technical clutter.
  • 📖 View Details: Click any certificate to view its expiration date, issuer, and other important information in plain language.
  • 💾 Export Any Certificate: Save certificates in three common formats: DER, PEM, and PKCS#12. Perfect for backups, sharing, or other programs.
  • 🛡️ 100% Safe & Read-Only: This tool never modifies or deletes anything. You only look and export. Your system stays exactly as it is.

❓ Quick Questions Answered

Is it really safe?

Yes. The tool only reads information from your system. It cannot alter, delete, or disable any certificates. It's a "look and save" tool, nothing more.

What is DER, PEM, and PKCS#12?

These are simply different "envelope" formats for saving certificate data. Think of it like saving a document as .docx, .pdf, or .txt — different programs like different formats. This tool supports all three, so you're ready for anything.

Will this cost me anything?

No. It's completely free, with no subscriptions, ads, or hidden fees.

Why would I need to view certificates?

Common reasons include: troubleshooting internet issues, checking if security updates are needed, backing up certificates before changing computers, or simply being curious about what your system has. With this tool, you can finally see what's there.
